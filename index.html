Good to know

Tenerife questions, answered

Which part of Tenerife should I stay in?

Most first-time visitors stay in the south-west, which is the sunniest and driest part of the island and home to the main beach resorts. Within the south, Costa Adeje is the upmarket, calmer choice, Playa de las Américas is best for nightlife, and Los Cristianos keeps a more local town feel. The green north around Puerto de la Cruz is cooler, lusher and more Spanish in character. Our south guide breaks the resorts down in detail.

Is the south or the north of Tenerife better?

They are genuinely different holidays. The south gets far more sunshine and calm beach weather year-round, which is why it has the big resorts. The north is greener, cooler and rainier, with more traditional towns, dramatic scenery and the historic city of La Laguna. Many people base in the south and take day trips north to Teide, Loro Parque and the countryside.

When is the best time to visit Tenerife?

The south is warm and dry all year, so there is no bad time. May, June, September and October give you excellent weather with fewer crowds and lower prices, while July, August, Christmas and Carnival in February are the busiest and most expensive.

Do I need a hire car in Tenerife?

Not for a beach-and-resort holiday in the south, where everything is walkable and buses and taxis are cheap. A car pays off if you want to explore Teide, the north or quieter corners on your own schedule.
