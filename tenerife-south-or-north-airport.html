Should you fly into Tenerife South or Tenerife North?

Home› Questions› Which airport

Short answer

Pick by where you are staying, not by fare alone. Tenerife South (TFS) is about twenty minutes from Los Cristianos and Costa Adeje and takes most international flights. Tenerife North (TFN) suits Puerto de la Cruz, Santa Cruz and the north, but is over an hour from the southern resorts.

Tenerife South (TFS, Reina Sofía)

This is the one most visitors use. It sits on the south-east coast near El Médano and handles the great majority of international and charter traffic, which is why the cheap package flights land here.

From arrivals it is roughly fifteen to twenty minutes to Los Cristianos and Playa de las Américas, and twenty to twenty-five to Costa Adeje. Taxis, pre-booked transfers, hire cars and TITSA buses all serve it, and if you are staying anywhere on the southern strip this is simply the right airport.

Tenerife North (TFN, Los Rodeos)

The northern airport sits just above La Laguna, about fifteen minutes from there and from Santa Cruz, and thirty to forty from Puerto de la Cruz. It handles most inter-island flights and a lot of mainland Spanish traffic, plus a smaller number of international routes.

One practical quirk: it sits at around 630 metres, high enough that low cloud rolls across it when the coast below is clear. Flights operate through it routinely, but if you land in mist having left sunshine in the south, that is why.

The trap is distance. TFN to Costa Adeje is well over an hour by road, and a transfer priced on that distance can wipe out whatever you saved on the fare.

How to choose in one line

Staying anywhere from Los Cristianos to Los Gigantes, fly into South. Staying in Puerto de la Cruz, La Orotava, La Laguna or Santa Cruz, fly into North if the schedule allows. Touring the whole island with a hire car, either works — pick on price and timing.

Two footnotes. If you are island-hopping, inter-island flights mostly use the north, so a trip combining Tenerife with La Palma or El Hierro may want TFN at one end. And if you are arriving late at night, the shorter transfer is worth more than it looks on paper — an extra hour in a minibus after a delayed flight is nobody's idea of a holiday.

  • Southern resorts → Tenerife South (TFS)
  • Puerto de la Cruz, La Laguna, Santa Cruz → North (TFN)
  • TFN is 1h+ from the southern resorts
  • Inter-island flights mostly use TFN
Also asked

Related questions

How far is Tenerife South Airport from Costa Adeje?

Roughly twenty to twenty-five minutes by road on the TF-1, and about fifteen to twenty to Los Cristianos.

Can you get from Tenerife North to the southern resorts?

Yes, but allow well over an hour by road. Bus, taxi, transfer and hire car are all available; the cost of the longer transfer often cancels out a cheaper flight.

Which airport is better for Puerto de la Cruz?

Tenerife North, at thirty to forty minutes, against roughly an hour and a quarter from the south.

Why is Tenerife North often cloudy?

It sits at around 630 metres on the north side, where trade-wind cloud banks up against the hills. The coast below can be clear while the airport is in mist.
