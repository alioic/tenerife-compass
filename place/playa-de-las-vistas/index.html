Playa de las Vistas

The safest, calmest big beach in the south, and the one to choose with small children.

Home› Places› South Tenerife› Playa de las Vistas

Playa de las Vistas stretches for over a kilometre between Los Cristianos and Playa de las Américas, and it is the most family-friendly beach on this coast. Breakwaters keep the water flat, the imported golden sand shelves gently, and everything you need is within a few steps of the promenade behind it.

What to see and do at Playa de las Vistas

Swimming is the point here: the sheltered water is calm enough for children and weak swimmers, and there are lifeguards through the season. The beach has good accessibility, including ramps and assisted bathing services. Behind it, the promenade runs into the old town of Los Cristianos in one direction, with the working harbour and its fish restaurants at the far end, and towards Las Américas in the other.

How to get to Playa de las Vistas

Fifteen to twenty minutes from Tenerife South Airport along the TF-1. The beach sits between the two resorts, so it is walkable from either. TITSA buses stop nearby, and the whole seafront from Los Cristianos to Costa Adeje is flat and easy on foot or with a pushchair.

Best time to go, and practical tips

The Los Cristianos end is generally quieter than the Las Américas end. It is one of the better-value spots on the coast for eating, since the old town behind it has plenty of local restaurants a street or two back from the sand. Sunday mornings bring a market near the harbour.

Good to know

Playa de las Vistas FAQ

Is Playa de las Vistas safe for swimming?

It is among the safest big beaches in Tenerife. Breakwaters keep the water calm and there are lifeguards in season. Always follow the flags, as Atlantic conditions can still change.

Is Playa de las Vistas better than Playa del Duque?

For families and swimming, generally yes: it is calmer and cheaper. Playa del Duque is smarter and quieter, with better facilities at the upmarket end.
