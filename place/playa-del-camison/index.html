Playa del Camisón

The nicest beach in Playa de las Américas: small, sheltered and a clear step above its neighbours.

Home› Places› South Tenerife› Playa del Camisón

Playa del Camisón is a compact crescent of golden sand at the quieter end of Playa de las Américas, tucked behind breakwaters that keep the water flat. It is smaller than Playa de Troya up the coast and considerably more pleasant, which is why regulars to this resort tend to walk past two other beaches to get to it.

What to see and do at Playa del Camisón

It is a beach for swimming and sitting rather than doing. The sheltered water suits children and weaker swimmers, there are showers, sunbeds and lifeguards in season, and the palm-lined promenade runs directly behind. From here you can walk south into Los Cristianos in about twenty minutes or north through Las Américas towards Costa Adeje.

How to get to Playa del Camisón

Fifteen to twenty minutes from Tenerife South Airport on the TF-1, at the southern end of Playa de las Américas near the Mare Nostrum complex. It is walkable from anywhere in the resort and from Los Cristianos along the seafront.

Best time to go, and practical tips

Because it is small it fills early, so mornings are better in high season. It is a good choice if you are staying in Las Américas with children but want to avoid the busier central beaches. The promenade here is one of the better evening walks on this coast, and it is flat the whole way.

Good to know

Playa del Camisón FAQ

Which is the best beach in Playa de las Américas?

Playa del Camisón is generally reckoned the nicest: it is smaller, more sheltered and quieter than Playa de Troya. It is at the Los Cristianos end of the resort.

Is Playa del Camisón good for children?

Yes. Breakwaters keep the water calm, the sand shelves gently and there are lifeguards in season.
