Cruz del Carmen

The gateway to the Anaga laurel forest, with a visitor centre, a mirador and the easiest trails into the woods.

Home› Places› Santa Cruz & La Laguna› Cruz del Carmen

Cruz del Carmen sits on the Anaga ridge above La Laguna and is where most people start exploring the range. There is a visitor centre, a viewpoint over the valley and, crucially, the trailheads into the laurisilva, an ancient laurel forest that has survived here since before the last ice age.

What to see and do at Cruz del Carmen

The Sendero de los Sentidos, the path of the senses, is a set of short, easy loops from the car park that take between half an hour and just over an hour and give you the forest without a serious hike. Longer routes run from here down towards Punta del Hidalgo or across to Taganana, taking three to five hours. The mirador beside the centre looks back over the La Laguna plain when the cloud allows.

How to get to Cruz del Carmen

About thirty minutes from La Laguna or Santa Cruz on the TF-12, a winding but manageable mountain road. There is a car park at the visitor centre which fills at weekends, so arrive early. Buses into Anaga are sparse, so a car or a guided tour is realistic.

Best time to go, and practical tips

Expect mist and drizzle on the ridge even when the coast is in full sun. That cloud is exactly what feeds the forest, so treat it as part of the experience and bring a layer and grippy shoes. Note that some protected routes, such as the Bosque Encantado at El Pijaral, require a free permit booked in advance.

Good to know

Cruz del Carmen FAQ

Do you need a permit to hike in Anaga?

Not for most trails, including the Sendero de los Sentidos. Some protected routes such as the Bosque Encantado at El Pijaral do require a free permit booked in advance.

Is Anaga always misty?

Often, on the ridge. Trade-wind cloud sits against these mountains and is what keeps the laurel forest alive, so plan for it rather than against it.
