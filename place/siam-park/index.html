Siam Park

Repeatedly voted the world's best water park, and the south's single strongest attraction.

Home› Places› South Tenerife› Siam Park

Siam Park sits on the hillside above Costa Adeje, an elaborately Thai-themed water park that has topped traveller polls for years. It is bigger, cleaner and better landscaped than the genre usually manages, and it comfortably fills a full day.

Full guide

We have a dedicated guide to Siam Park with prices, booking details and an honest verdict. Read it here.

What to see and do at Siam Park

The headline ride is the Tower of Power, a near-vertical drop that fires you through a tube running inside a shark aquarium. Beyond it there is the Dragon, the Singha rollercoaster-style slide, a wave pool that produces genuinely large waves, the Mai Thai River lazy river, and the Lost City play area for younger children. Siam Beach gives you white sand and loungers when you have had enough of the queues.

How to get to Siam Park

A short drive above Costa Adeje, roughly twenty minutes from Tenerife South Airport, with a free shuttle bus looping the main southern resorts. Parking is available on site.

Best time to go, and practical tips

Go early: the queues build sharply from late morning and the park is at its best in the first two hours. In peak season the fast-pass upgrade genuinely saves time rather than just money. Height restrictions apply on the bigger rides, so check before promising anything to a small child. Bring a towel and expect to pay for lockers.

Good to know

Siam Park FAQ

How much does Siam Park cost?

An adult day ticket starts at around forty euros, with children cheaper and a twin ticket with Loro Parque at roughly seventy. Booking online is normally cheaper than at the gate.

Is Siam Park worth it?

For most visitors, yes. It is the strongest single attraction in the south and the standard is unusually high. Go early and consider the fast pass in peak season.
