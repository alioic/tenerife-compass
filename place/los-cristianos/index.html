Los Cristianos

A former fishing village that grew into a resort without losing its town centre, and the island's ferry port for La Gomera.

Home› Places› South Tenerife› Los Cristianos

Los Cristianos sits at the southern end of the resort strip and is the most lived-in of them. There is still an old town behind the harbour, a Sunday market, and streets where the businesses serve residents rather than visitors. It is flatter and more walkable than its neighbours, which is one reason it is popular with older travellers and with visitors who have mobility needs.

What to see and do in Los Cristianos

Two beaches: Playa de los Cristianos by the harbour, and the far bigger Playa de las Vistas along the promenade. The working port is the departure point for ferries to La Gomera, La Palma and El Hierro, which makes a day trip to La Gomera genuinely easy. Whale-watching boats leave from the same harbour. The promenade runs unbroken to Playa de las Américas and on to Costa Adeje, so evenings tend to involve a long flat walk and dinner somewhere along it.

How to get to Los Cristianos

Fifteen to twenty minutes from Tenerife South Airport on the TF-1. It is the main southern hub for TITSA buses, including the 111 and 110 express services to Santa Cruz, so it is the easiest southern base if you are not hiring a car.

Best time to go, and practical tips

For eating, walk a street or two back from the seafront where the prices drop and the cooking improves. The Sunday morning market near the harbour is worth timing your week around. If you want nightlife, this is the quiet end — Playa de las Américas is a twenty-minute walk north.

Good to know

Los Cristianos FAQ

Is Los Cristianos good for older visitors?

It is one of the best choices on the island. The seafront is flat and continuous, the beaches have assisted bathing services, and the town has a real centre rather than only bars and tourist shops.

Can you get to La Gomera from Los Cristianos?

Yes. Ferries run from the harbour several times a day and the crossing takes under an hour, which makes a day trip realistic.
