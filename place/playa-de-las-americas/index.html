Playa de las Américas

The island's nightlife capital, cheaper than its neighbours and unapologetic about what it is.

Home› Places› South Tenerife› Playa de las Américas

Playa de las Américas is the busiest and loudest of the southern resorts, built in the 1970s and 80s with no pretence of being a Canarian town. What it offers is a run of decent beaches, a huge choice of cheap places to eat and drink, and the island's only real concentration of nightlife.

What to see and do in Playa de las Américas

Playa de Troya and Playa del Camisón are the main beaches, the latter smaller, more sheltered and generally the nicer of the two. The promenade connects everything and runs both north into Costa Adeje and south into Los Cristianos. The nightlife clusters around the Veronicas strip, which is exactly as advertised and easily avoided if it is not your thing — a few hundred metres away the resort is unremarkably quiet.

How to get to Playa de las Américas

Around fifteen to twenty minutes from Tenerife South Airport on the TF-1, with the same taxi, transfer and TITSA options as the other southern resorts. Everything within the resort is walkable.

Best time to go, and practical tips

Choose your street carefully. Hotels within earshot of the Veronicas strip are cheap for a reason, and the difference between a noisy block and a quiet one can be two hundred metres. Playa del Camisón is the beach to pick with children. If you want value without the volume, look at Los Cristianos instead.

Good to know

Playa de las Américas FAQ

Is Playa de las Américas suitable for families?

Parts of it are. The beaches are good and Playa del Camisón in particular suits children. Choose a hotel well away from the Veronicas nightlife strip and check reviews for noise.

Is Playa de las Américas cheaper than Costa Adeje?

Yes, noticeably, for both hotels and eating out. The beaches and hotel standards are a step below, and it is much livelier at night.
