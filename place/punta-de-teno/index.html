Punta de Teno

The island's western tip: a lighthouse, an empty headland and the finest view of the Los Gigantes cliffs.

Home› Places› West Tenerife› Punta de Teno

Punta de Teno is the westernmost point of Tenerife, a low volcanic headland with a red-and-white lighthouse at the end of it. There is nothing there but rock, sea and the view — south along the full wall of the Los Gigantes cliffs and out to La Gomera and La Palma on a clear day. Getting there is deliberately restricted, which is why it stays as good as it is.

What to see and do at Punta de Teno

The lighthouse and the headland around it, and the view. There is a small rocky cove for a swim on calm days, and the drive or ride in along the Teno cliffs is spectacular in its own right. Most visitors stay an hour. Sunset here is exceptional and is the reason the last shuttle back matters.

How to get to Punta de Teno

Private cars are barred from the access road during the main part of the day, so nearly everyone arrives on the TITSA shuttle bus from Buenavista del Norte, a short and cheap ride. Check current times and any restrictions before you set off: the road runs beneath unstable cliffs and has closed for long spells after rockfalls, and the rules change accordingly.

Best time to go, and practical tips

Take water and a hat — there is no shade and no café at the point. The wind here is stronger than the temperature suggests. Confirm the shuttle timetable and the road's status on the day rather than relying on an older guide, since this is the single most changeable access arrangement on the island.

Good to know

Punta de Teno FAQ

Can you drive to Punta de Teno?

Generally not during the day. Private vehicles are restricted and visitors use the TITSA shuttle from Buenavista del Norte. Always check the current rules, as the access road closes after rockfalls.

Is Punta de Teno worth the trip?

For the view along the Los Gigantes cliffs, yes. It is an hour at the point plus a spectacular ride in, and it is one of the emptiest places on an otherwise busy island.
