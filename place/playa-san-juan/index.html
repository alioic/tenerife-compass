Playa San Juan

A working coastal town on the west coast, quiet, Canarian and largely undiscovered by the resorts.

Home› Places› West Tenerife› Playa San Juan

Playa San Juan sits on the west coast in the municipality of Guía de Isora, a former fishing and tomato-export port that has grown into a low-key town rather than a resort. There is a harbour, a dark-sand beach, a seafront promenade and very little that exists purely for visitors, which is exactly why people who find it tend to come back.

What to see and do in Playa San Juan

The beach and the harbour are the centre of it. The sand is dark and the water is usually calmer than the coast further north, and there is a promenade with unfussy restaurants where the prices are noticeably below Costa Adeje. La Gomera sits on the horizon and the sunsets from here are among the best on the island. There are no attractions as such; the point is a slow day.

How to get to Playa San Juan

Roughly thirty minutes north of Costa Adeje on the TF-47, and about forty-five minutes from Tenerife South Airport. TITSA buses run along this coast between Los Cristianos and Los Gigantes. Parking is straightforward outside peak weekends.

Best time to go, and practical tips

Come for the quiet rather than for things to do. It suits couples, walkers and anyone who wants a Canarian town with a beach attached rather than a resort. The west coast faces the sunset, so evening is the time to be on the front. There are some very smart hotels on the hillside above this stretch, which means the town has better restaurants than its size suggests.

Playa San Juan FAQ

Is Playa San Juan a good place to stay?

For quiet, yes. It suits couples and older visitors who want a real town, good food and a beach, without nightlife or crowds. Anyone wanting resort facilities should look at Costa Adeje.

Can you swim at Playa San Juan?

Usually. The bay is more sheltered than much of the west coast, though this is still the open Atlantic — follow the flags.
