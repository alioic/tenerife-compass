Playa de la Arena

A compact beach of jet-black volcanic sand that has flown the Blue Flag for over twenty-five years.

Home› Places› West Tenerife› Playa de la Arena

Playa de la Arena is the main beach of the western resort strip, a small, tidy bay of genuinely black volcanic sand beneath the shoulder of the Los Gigantes cliffs. It is well serviced, easy to reach and popular, and it is the best swimming beach on this coast when conditions allow.

What to see and do at Playa de la Arena

The beach is compact, so it can feel busy in high season, but the facilities are good: loungers, showers and lifeguards, with bars and restaurants directly behind. The coastal walkway links it with Puerto de Santiago and on towards Los Gigantes marina, which makes an easy stroll and a good way to reach the boat trips.

How to get to Playa de la Arena

Roughly twenty-five to thirty-five minutes from Costa Adeje via the TF-1 and TF-47, and about forty-five minutes from Tenerife South Airport.

Best time to go, and practical tips

Respect the red flags. The swell on this coast is real and the beach is closed to swimming more often than the sheltered southern bays. Black sand gets genuinely hot underfoot in the middle of the day, so bring something for your feet. The whole coast faces west, so stay for the sunset.

Good to know

Playa de la Arena FAQ

Is Playa de la Arena safe for swimming?

On calm days yes, and it is the best swimming beach on this stretch. The Atlantic swell here is real, though, so always follow the flags.

Why is the sand black at Playa de la Arena?

It is natural volcanic sand, which is what most of the island's beaches look like away from the imported golden sand of the south.
