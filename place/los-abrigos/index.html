Los Abrigos

A working fishing village ten minutes from the airport, and the south's best-value place to eat fish.

Home› Places› El Médano & the east› Los Abrigos

Los Abrigos is a small, low-rise village on the south-east coast that has done very well out of one thing: fish. A row of restaurants along the seafront serves what the boats bring in, and Canarians drive down from across the island to eat there. It is not a beach destination and makes no attempt to be one.

What to see and do in Los Abrigos

Eating is the reason to come. The seafront restaurants are unpretentious and the fish is priced by weight, which is normal here and worth knowing before you order. Beyond that there is a small harbour, a set of natural rock pools along the coast to the east, and a walkable stretch of shoreline. Most visits last an evening.

How to get to Los Abrigos

About ten minutes from Tenerife South Airport, which makes it a genuinely good first or last meal of a trip. Roughly twenty minutes from Los Cristianos on the TF-1, and TITSA line 470 links it with El Médano, Golf del Sur and Los Cristianos. Parking on the seafront is tight at dinner.

Best time to go, and practical tips

Book at weekends, when locals fill the tables. Fish is sold by weight, so ask the price per kilo and roughly what your fish weighs before agreeing — nobody minds the question. There is no sandy beach here; La Tejita and El Médano are ten minutes away if you want one. The village is exposed to the same trade winds as that coast.

Good to know

Los Abrigos FAQ

Why is Los Abrigos known for fish?

It is a working fishing village, and its seafront restaurants sell the local catch simply cooked and priced by weight. It has a strong reputation among Canarians, not just visitors.

Is Los Abrigos worth visiting?

For a meal, yes, especially on your first or last night given it is ten minutes from the airport. There is no beach, so it is an evening rather than a day.
