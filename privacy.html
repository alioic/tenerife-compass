Privacy, cookies & affiliate disclosure

This page explains, in plain language, how Tenerife Compass handles your data and how the site makes money. Last updated July 2026.

Affiliate disclosure

Tenerife Compass is free to read and is funded through affiliate partnerships. That means some of the links on this site, particularly booking buttons for hotels, tours and car hire, are affiliate links. If you click one and go on to book, we may earn a small commission from the provider. It does not cost you a penny more, and the price you pay is exactly the same as booking directly.

This is important to us: a commission never buys a place on this site. We choose and rank what we recommend on merit alone, based on our own research. If somewhere is not worth it, we say so, whether or not we could earn from it. Affiliate links are marked so browsers and search engines can identify them.

In short

You get honest recommendations. We may earn a commission if you book through our links, at no extra cost to you. What we recommend is never for sale.

Cookies

This site uses a small number of cookies. Essential cookies keep the site working. If we use analytics cookies to understand which pages are useful, they are set only with your consent, and you can decline non-essential cookies at any time. Affiliate partners may also set a cookie when you click a booking link, so the provider can credit the referral if you book.

What data we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect names, addresses or payment details, because no bookings happen on this site. Any booking you make happens on the partner's own secure website under their terms and privacy policy. If you email us, we use your message only to reply to you.

Third-party sites

When you follow a link to a booking partner such as Booking.com, GetYourGuide or a car-hire provider, you leave Tenerife Compass and their privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for the content or practices of other websites.

Your choices

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. If you have any question about privacy or this disclosure, email hello@tenerifecompass.com.
