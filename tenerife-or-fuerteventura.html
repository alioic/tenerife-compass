Choosing your trip

Tenerife or Fuerteventura — which should you choose?

Home› Questions› Tenerife or Fuerteventura

Short answer

Fuerteventura has the best beaches in the Canaries and the most reliable wind, and very little else. Tenerife has Teide, forests, cities and far more to do on a day you do not want to lie on sand. Choose by whether the beach is the holiday or part of it.

The beaches are not a close call

Fuerteventura wins this decisively. It is the oldest and flattest of the main islands, and erosion has given it kilometres of pale sand: the dunes at Corralejo in the north, the vast Sotavento lagoon on the Jandía peninsula, and the wild, undeveloped sweep of Cofete on the far side. These are genuinely among the best beaches in Europe.

Tenerife's beaches are good rather than exceptional. The golden sand in the south was largely imported, and the island's natural coast is black volcanic sand, often on exposed Atlantic shoreline. If your holiday is measured in hours on a beach, Fuerteventura is the better island and it is not particularly close.

Everything else points the other way

Fuerteventura is arid, flat and nearly treeless. That starkness is beautiful, and it is also the whole menu — the island has few attractions, modest nightlife and long empty drives between resorts. A car is close to essential and rain is rare enough that a wet day is not a planning concern.

Tenerife gives you Teide at 3,715 metres, the ancient laurel forest of Anaga, the Los Gigantes cliffs, two proper cities, the best attractions in the archipelago in Siam Park and Loro Parque, and resident whales offshore year-round. When you do not feel like the beach, there is somewhere to go.

Wind, and who each island suits

Fuerteventura is consistently windy, which is why Sotavento is a world-class windsurfing and kitesurfing venue. If you ride, that settles it. If you want to read a book on a towel, it is a genuine drawback — beach windbreaks are standard equipment there for a reason. Tenerife's south-west coast is more sheltered, with El Médano the windy exception.

In short: Fuerteventura for beach purists, watersports and quiet. Tenerife for variety, families who need rainy-day options, walkers, and anyone who would be restless with sand and not much else. Both are warm all year and cost broadly the same.

  • Fuerteventura: better beaches, reliable wind, quiet
  • Tenerife: Teide, forests, cities, attractions
  • Fuerteventura is flat, arid and needs a car
  • Similar climate and cost either way
Also asked

Related questions

Which has better beaches, Tenerife or Fuerteventura?

Fuerteventura, clearly. Corralejo, Sotavento and Cofete are among the best beaches in Europe. Tenerife's southern beaches are good but largely imported sand.

Is Fuerteventura boring?

It depends what you want. It has few attractions and quiet nights, which is the appeal for some and the drawback for others. Tenerife has considerably more to do.

Which is better for families?

Tenerife, generally — Siam Park, Loro Parque and sheltered lifeguarded beaches, plus somewhere to go on an off day. Fuerteventura suits families happy to spend the week on sand.

Is Fuerteventura windier than Tenerife?

Yes, noticeably and consistently. It is excellent for watersports and less so for still days on a towel.
