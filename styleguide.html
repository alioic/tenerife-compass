FAQ accordion (with FAQ schema)
Do I need a hire car in South Tenerife?
Not for a resort holiday — the main resorts are walkable and well served by buses and taxis. A car helps for exploring Teide and the north.
When is the best time to visit?
The south is warm and dry all year. May–June and September–October offer great weather with lower prices.
