About us

An honest guide to Tenerife

Tenerife Compass is an independent travel guide to Tenerife. We are not a tour operator or a hotel chain, and we are not trying to sell you a package. The goal is simple: help you understand the island, choose the right base, and spend your time and money on the things that are genuinely worth it.

Why we started

Search for a Tenerife hotel or a whale-watching trip and you drown in near-identical pages that all say the same thing. We wanted a guide that actually takes a position: which resort suits which kind of traveller, which tours are worth the price, and which are a waste of a good beach day. We start with the sunny south, where most people stay, and we are adding the rest of the island region by region.

How we keep it honest

We research every place we list and keep prices and details current. When something is overpriced, crowded or overhyped, we say so. Our recommendations are never paid placements. We do earn a commission when you book some things through our links, but that never changes what we recommend or the order we recommend it in. You can read the full affiliate disclosure here.

Get in touch

Spotted something out of date, or think we have missed a gem? We would genuinely like to hear it. Email us at hello@tenerifecompass.com and we will take a look.
