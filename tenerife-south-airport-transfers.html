Tenerife South Airport to your resort

Every southern resort sits 15 to 25 minutes from Tenerife South Airport along the free TF-1 motorway, so a taxi costs roughly €25 to €45, a pre-booked private transfer about €35 to €50 per car, and the TITSA bus around €3 to €5 a head.

Home› Airport transfers

The short answer

Quickest: a taxi from the rank outside arrivals, or a private transfer with a driver already waiting for you. Both are door to door and take 15 to 25 minutes. Cheapest by a wide margin: the TITSA public bus, roughly €3 to €5 for a journey of about 30 to 45 minutes. If you are two adults with hand luggage, the bus is easy. If you are four people with suitcases at midnight, take the taxi and stop thinking about it.

Tenerife South (TFS, officially Reina Sofía) is the airport almost every holiday flight uses, and it was built in the right place: the whole resort strip is a straight run west along the TF-1. There are no tolls anywhere on Tenerife, the motorway is in good condition, and traffic only really bites on weekday mornings around Los Cristianos.

Arrivals is small enough that you cannot get lost: the taxi rank, the bus stop and the car hire desks are all a couple of minutes from the exit doors.

Your options

Four ways to make the journey

All prices are approximate and one way. They move with season, time of day and how many of you there are.

Taxi

The rank sits directly outside arrivals and is staffed even on late flights. Canarian taxis are white, metered, and show a green light when free. Expect supplements for the airport pick-up and for luggage. Ask the driver for the approximate fare before you set off, which is normal here and avoids any surprise at the other end.

From ~€25 · €25–40 Los Cristianos, €30–45 Costa Adeje

Private transfer

Booked before you fly, priced per vehicle rather than per person, and the driver is standing in arrivals with your name when you walk out. Worth it for families, for late arrivals, and for anyone who does not want to negotiate anything after a four-hour flight. A minibus for six costs roughly €70 to €110.

From ~€35 · per car, up to about 3–4 people

Shared shuttle

A minibus that fills with passengers heading to different hotels, then works its way along the coast dropping people off. Cheaper per head than a taxi if you are travelling solo or as a couple, but you may sit through several other hotels before yours, and you will usually wait for the vehicle to fill.

From ~€15 · per person, slower than the alternatives

TITSA public bus

The green guaguas stop right outside the terminal and run frequently to all the southern resorts. Cheapest option on the island by some distance, clean and reliable, with luggage space underneath. The catch is a short walk with your bags at the far end, since the bus drops you at a stop rather than your reception desk.

From ~€3 · about 30–45 minutes

The cheap way

Taking the TITSA bus from TFS

Four lines matter for a southern arrival. Learn the numbers and you will never overpay for a journey on this island.

  • Line 111 is the one most people want: airport to Costa Adeje and on to Santa Cruz, serving Los Cristianos and Playa de las Américas along the way. Fast and frequent.
  • Line 343 is the express that links both airports with Puerto de la Cruz in the north. Useful if you have landed at the wrong airport, or if you are staying north but flying into TFS.
  • Lines 40 and 711 handle local runs to Los Cristianos, Las Américas and Costa Adeje. The 711 is the night service, which matters if your flight lands after everything else has stopped.

Single fares run roughly €3 to €5 depending on where you are going and whether you pay cash or tap a card. The Ten+ (tenmás) contactless card costs about €2 as a one-off plus whatever you load onto it, can be shared between everyone in your group by tapping once per passenger, and cuts fares by around 20 to 30%. If you plan to use the bus more than a couple of times during the week, buy one on arrival and it pays for itself almost immediately.

Honest limitation: buses drop you at a stop on the main road, and some Costa Adeje hotels are a genuine uphill walk from there. Check where your hotel actually sits before you commit to the bus with a large suitcase.

Worth knowing

What if you land at Tenerife North?

Tenerife North (TFN, also called Los Rodeos) is a completely separate airport near La Laguna at the other end of the island, roughly 70 to 75 km and over an hour from the southern resorts. It handles most inter-island and mainland Spanish flights. For a holiday in the south, fly into TFS if you have the choice, because a cheaper fare into TFN is usually cancelled out by the transfer.

If you do land there, TITSA line 343 runs south, and a taxi or private transfer will cost considerably more than the same trip from TFS.

Another option

Picking up a hire car instead

If you intend to see Teide, Masca or the northern towns, collecting a car at the airport turns your transfer into the first leg of the holiday. Daily rates are about €10 to €15 in low season such as June, average out around €20 to €25 across the year, and climb to around €35 or more a day in the peak weeks around Christmas, Easter and August.

Two things to check before you book: the fuel policy, where full-to-full is the one you want, and whether excess-waiver insurance is included or sold as an extra at the desk. Roads across the island are good and there are no tolls anywhere on Tenerife. More detail in our getting around guide.

Good to know

Airport transfer FAQ

How much is a taxi from Tenerife South Airport to Costa Adeje?

Roughly €30 to €45 one way, with the meter running plus supplements for the airport pick-up and luggage. Los Cristianos is a little closer and typically comes in at €25 to €40. Ask the driver for an approximate fare before you set off.

How long does it take to get from TFS to Playa de las Américas?

About 15 to 20 minutes by road, covering 16 to 18 km on the TF-1 motorway. Costa Adeje is slightly further at 20 to 25 minutes. The TITSA bus takes longer, around 30 to 45 minutes, because it stops along the way.

Is there a bus from Tenerife South Airport to the resorts?

Yes. TITSA line 111 runs from the airport to Costa Adeje via Los Cristianos and Playa de las Américas, and lines 40 and 711 cover local runs, with the 711 operating at night. Single fares are roughly €3 to €5, and the stop is just outside the terminal.

Is a private transfer worth it over a taxi?

Often, yes. A private transfer costs about €35 to €50 per car, which is close to taxi money, but the price is fixed before you fly and the driver is waiting in arrivals. For six people a minibus runs roughly €70 to €110, which beats two taxis.

Can I get to the southern resorts from Tenerife North Airport?

You can, but it is 70 to 75 km and over an hour by road. TITSA line 343 connects the two airports and Puerto de la Cruz. For a holiday in the south, flying into Tenerife South is almost always the better choice.
