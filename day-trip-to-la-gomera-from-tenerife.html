Choosing your trip

Can you do a day trip to La Gomera from Tenerife?

Home› Questions› La Gomera day trip

Short answer

Yes. Fast ferries from Los Cristianos reach San Sebastián de la Gomera in under an hour, several times a day, which makes a day trip entirely practical. It is a long day and you will only scratch the island — but the ferry is the easy part, and getting around once you land is what needs planning.

Getting there is the simple bit

Ferries leave from the harbour at Los Cristianos, which is also where the whale-watching boats go from. The fast catamaran crossing to San Sebastián takes under an hour, and there are several departures a day in each direction, so an early boat out and a late one back is a realistic plan.

More than one operator runs the route, at different speeds and prices. Book ahead in peak season and at weekends, and give yourself a margin at the Tenerife end — the harbour car park fills and the check-in cut-off is real.

The hire-car trap

This catches people out every year, so it is worth stating plainly: most Tenerife hire-car agreements do not permit taking the vehicle to another island. Turning up at the ferry with a rental car and no permission can void your insurance entirely. Read your contract, and if you want a car on La Gomera, either clear it with the company in advance or book one to collect in San Sebastián.

The alternative is an organised excursion sold from the southern resorts, which bundles the ferry with a coach tour and removes the problem. For a first visit that is the low-friction option. Public buses on La Gomera exist but are infrequent and will not get you around the island in a day.

What you can realistically see

San Sebastián itself is a pleasant hour or two: a small harbour town where Columbus took on water before crossing the Atlantic in 1492, a fact the town has not forgotten. Beyond it, the island climbs fast and the roads are slow and continuously winding — distances that look short on a map take much longer than you expect.

The single thing most worth the drive is Garajonay National Park, a UNESCO-listed laurel forest that survives from a time when this vegetation covered much of southern Europe. It is misty, ancient and completely unlike anywhere in Tenerife's south. Valle Gran Rey on the west coast is the other classic, though it is a long way for a day trip. You may also come across a demonstration of silbo, the whistled form of Spanish used to talk across the island's ravines and recognised by UNESCO.

Honest verdict: one day gives you a taste and a long ferry day. La Gomera rewards an overnight far more than it rewards a dash.

  • Under an hour by fast ferry from Los Cristianos
  • Garajonay laurel forest is the highlight
  • Check your hire-car contract before boarding
  • An overnight beats a day trip if you can
Also asked

Related questions

How long is the ferry from Tenerife to La Gomera?

Under an hour on the fast catamaran from Los Cristianos to San Sebastián. Slower services take longer and usually cost less.

Can you take a hire car from Tenerife to La Gomera?

Usually not. Most Tenerife rental agreements forbid inter-island travel and doing it anyway can void your insurance. Check your contract, or hire a car on La Gomera instead.

Is a day trip to La Gomera worth it?

Yes if you accept it is a taste rather than a tour. Garajonay National Park is the reason to go. If you can spare a night, the island is far better over two days.

What is silbo?

Silbo Gomero, a whistled form of Spanish developed to carry across La Gomera's deep ravines. It is recognised by UNESCO and taught in the island's schools.
