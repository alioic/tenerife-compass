Weather and safety

Can you swim in the sea in Tenerife in winter?

Home› Questions› Winter swimming

Short answer

Yes, and plenty of people do. The sea around Tenerife drops to roughly 19°C at its coldest in late winter and reaches about 23°C in early autumn. Most northern Europeans find 19°C perfectly swimmable; if you are used to Mediterranean summers, it will feel brisk for the first minute.

How cold it actually gets

The Atlantic here is remarkably stable. The coldest water comes in February, March and April, sitting somewhere around nineteen degrees, and the warmest in September and October at roughly twenty-three. That is a narrower swing than most of Europe manages, and it is why the island sells itself on year-round swimming rather than a summer season.

Nineteen degrees is colder than a heated pool and warmer than the sea off Britain, Ireland or Scandinavia in any month. Whether that reads as pleasant or bracing depends entirely on what you are used to. Children tend to mind it less than adults.

Which coast, and which beach

This matters more than the temperature. The sheltered southern beaches — Playa de las Vistas, Playa del Camisón, Playa del Duque, Fañabé — sit behind breakwaters that keep the water flat, and they are swimmable through the winter on most days.

The north and west are a different story in winter. Atlantic swell builds from roughly October through March, and beaches such as Playa Jardín, El Bollullo and the Anaga coast are frequently rough enough to be closed to swimming. This is the season when the seawater lidos at Lago Martiánez and the rock pools at Garachico and Punta del Hidalgo either come into their own or close entirely, depending on the day.

The pool question nobody asks in time

If you are travelling between November and March and a pool matters to you, check whether the hotel's pool is heated before you book, not after. Many southern hotels heat theirs through the winter and advertise it; plenty do not, and an unheated pool in January is noticeably colder than the sea.

The other winter reality is wind rather than water. The trade winds ease in winter compared with summer, which is good news for the beach, but the sun sits lower and drops sooner. Afternoons on the sand are shorter than the temperature suggests.

  • Around 19°C at the winter low, 23°C at the autumn high
  • Sheltered southern beaches swim through winter
  • North and west often too rough from October
  • Check whether the hotel pool is heated
Also asked

Related questions

What is the sea temperature in Tenerife in December?

Around twenty degrees, easing towards nineteen through February and March. It is one of the warmest sea temperatures available in Europe in winter.

Is Tenerife warm enough to swim in January?

For most northern European visitors, yes, on the sheltered southern beaches. The water is cooler than in summer but the swing across the year is small.

Which beaches are best for winter swimming in Tenerife?

The breakwater-sheltered southern beaches: Playa de las Vistas, Playa del Camisón, Playa del Duque and Fañabé. Avoid the exposed north and west coasts, which are at their roughest in winter.
