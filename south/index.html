South Tenerife

Costa Adeje, Las Américas & Los Cristianos

The sunniest corner of the island, where most people spend their holiday. Here is where to stay, where to eat and what is genuinely worth booking, all mapped out.

The lie of the land

One coast, five distinct resorts

Locals lump it all together as "the south", but each resort has its own character. Here is the quick version, from the polished north end down to the working harbour town.

Upscale

Costa Adeje

The smart end: landscaped promenades, designer shops and the island's cluster of five-star hotels. Calm without being dull. Best for couples and families with a bit more to spend.

Where to stay here
Lively

Playa de las Américas

The island's nightlife capital, but with two sides. Bars and clubs around Verónicas, and a calmer, family-friendly stretch towards Playa del Camisón. Pick your spot with care.

Where to stay here
Best value

Los Cristianos

A former fishing town with a real old quarter, a working harbour and two sheltered beaches. More Spanish in feel, the best value of the three, and a favourite with families.

Where to stay here
Foodie · Quiet

La Caleta & El Duque

The exclusive end: glamour beaches, sea-view villas and the south's best seafood and Michelin dining. Tranquil and pricey, made for couples and food lovers.
